Drag & drop videos onto the panel. Drop one or more files — from anywhere,
not just your watched folders — and they're added to the top of the Videos
list, ready to compress. Dropped files behave exactly like recordings tamp
found on its own: click to run the default preset, expand for presets, a
preview, or a custom one-off conversion. The compressed file lands next to
the original, just like always.
